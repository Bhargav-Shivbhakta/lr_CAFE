# Photo pack 3: the 15 menu items still without a picture

Written to match the SDSU pack (pack 2) so the new images sit next to it without standing out. Each dish is built from the café's own menu description.

**How to use:** paste the shared style line, then the item line, into your image tool. Save each image as `<file name>.webp` (or .jpg), 1200 × 1200, and send them back together. I'll crop, check and import them, like pack 2. If the café has real photos of any of these, send those instead: a real photo always beats an illustration.

## Shared style line (paste first, every time)

> Photorealistic food photograph, square 1200 × 1200. One serving, centred, on a warm honey-brown wooden café table. Softly blurred café interior behind with wooden bistro chairs and warm lights. Warm natural window light, shallow depth of field, camera slightly above at about 30 degrees, the whole dish or cup in frame with a little table around it. Appetising, realistic portions. No people, no hands, no text, no watermark.

For **food**, add: *served on a round white ceramic plate.*
For **hot drinks**, add: *in a plain white ceramic mug with no logo or writing.*
For **iced drinks**, add: *in a clear plastic cup with ice, no logo or writing.*

(Pack 2 printed a made-up "Living Room" logo on its cups, which the café would need to approve. Plain cups avoid that. If the café sends its real cup artwork, we can match it later.)
