Rules that keep it premium

  • One cup, one spot. It lives in the top banner. Nothing ever plays over the food photos or the headline.
  • Real animation craft. It winds up before a move, squashes and stretches, and settles after. Nothing snaps.
  • Simple and readable. Each move is one clear idea in 2–3 steps, with props big enough to see at a glance.
  • Never repetitive. It never repeats a moment within its last three.
  • Calm by default. At most one big move every few minutes. At night only quiet moves, then it dozes.
  • Out of your way. Moments pause while you scroll or read, and stop when the app is in the background.
  • Holidays use the same library. On a holiday it changes outfits and holiday moments take over, so it all feels like one character.
  • Accessibility. With Reduce Motion on, it holds a still, friendly pose.
  • In the real app the cup would be built in Rive, which plays these as native animations on iOS and Android and switches moves on its own based on the time, your usual, your points and your order.
